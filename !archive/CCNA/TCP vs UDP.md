TCP
- connection-oriented
- reliable communication 
- sequencing (forward acknowledgement)
- flow control (ensure sender isnt overloading receiver)
- congestion control (ensure sender is not overloading network)
- 3-way handshake establishment (syn, synack, ack)
- 4-way handshake termination (fin, ack, fin, ack)

UDP
- not connection-oriented
- no reliable communication (best-effort)
- no sequencing
- no flow control
